About

Bioinformatician and clinical data scientist with a strong quantitative research background and hands-on experience in NGS and cancer genomics.

I work across WGS, WES, bulk RNA-seq and scRNA-seq, with practical expertise in GATK4/Mutect2, SAMtools, DESeq2, Seurat and Nextflow/nf-core, and I'm currently extending into spatial transcriptomics. I build reproducible, containerised workflows and bring years of clinical research experience to genomic and biomedical datasets.

My foundation is in epidemiology and biostatistics. Since 2016 I have analysed data for clinical investigators — cleaning and validating raw datasets, regression and survival modelling, predictive models, and study design and sample size planning — contributing to peer-reviewed publications and training postgraduate fellowship candidates in research methods.

At the MAI Lab I curate brain MRI data and evaluate segmentation models on African glioma scans — work driven by a simple conviction: African patients deserve to be well represented in the data that shapes precision medicine.

Flagship · Whole-genome sequencing

Somatic WGS Pipeline — HCC1395 Tumour–Normal, SEQC2 Benchmark

A modular 7-stage tumour–normal WGS pipeline on a 16 vCPU AWS instance, validated against the SEQC2 v1.2 gold-standard truth set. I debugged FASTQ read-name mismatches and cut runtime about 3× (48 h → 14 h) by moving to Colab Pro+; every stage can be re-run and audited on its own.

SEQC2 v1.2 48 h → 14 h 16 vCPU AWS 7 stages

Whole-exome sequencing

Somatic WES Pipeline — HCC1143 Breast Cancer

Docker-based GATK4 Best Practices pipeline targeting chr17 (TP53, BRCA1, ERBB2). FilterMutectCalls reduced 182 raw calls to 27 PASS variants and recovered the clonal TP53 p.Arg248Gln driver; C>T transitions (52%) are consistent with SBS1/SBS5 ageing signatures.

99.47% mapped 1.11% contamination TP53 VAF 98.6%

Single-cell RNA-seq

Breast Cancer Tumour Microenvironment (GSE176078)

Containerised Seurat v5 pipeline (Harmony, scDblFinder, SingleR, sccomp, pseudobulk DESeq2) across 9 patients and 3 subtypes. Fibroblasts were elevated in TNBC, and 9 T cell subclusters were resolved.

Bulk RNA-seq · Survival

TCGA-BRCA — PAM50 Basal-like vs Luminal A

752 tumours pulled through GDC/TCGAbiolinks. DESeq2 with apeglm shrinkage identified 5,346 differentially expressed genes, followed by GO/KEGG and GSEA enrichment, and Kaplan–Meier and Cox survival modelling.
