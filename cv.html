Key projects

Somatic WGS Pipeline — HCC1395 Tumour–Normal, SEQC2 Benchmark

github.com/Ceefour18/Breast_cancer_WGS · case study
  • Modular 7-stage tumour–normal WGS pipeline (FastQC → BWA-MEM → BQSR → Mutect2 → SnpEff) on 16 vCPU AWS, validated against the SEQC2 v1.2 gold-standard truth set.
  • Debugged FASTQ read-name mismatches and cut runtime ~3× (48 h → 14 h) by migrating to Colab Pro+; each stage independently re-runnable and auditable.

Somatic WES Pipeline — HCC1143 Breast Cancer Cell Line

github.com/Ceefour18/somatic-wes-breast-cancer-gatk4
  • Docker-based GATK4 Best Practices pipeline targeting chr17 (TP53, BRCA1, ERBB2): 99.47% reads mapped, 1.11% contamination; FilterMutectCalls reduced 182 raw calls to 27 PASS variants.
  • Recovered the clonal TP53 p.Arg248Gln driver (VAF 98.6%); C>T transitions (52%) consistent with SBS1/SBS5 ageing signatures. Tools: BWA-MEM, GATK4, SAMtools, SnpEff, Docker, Python.

TCGA-BRCA RNA-seq — PAM50 Basal-like vs Luminal A

github.com/Ceefour18/tcga-brca-rnaseq
  • 752 tumours via GDC/TCGAbiolinks; DESeq2 + apeglm identified 5,346 DEGs; GO/KEGG and GSEA enrichment; Kaplan–Meier and Cox survival modelling.

scRNA-seq Breast Cancer Tumour Microenvironment (GSE176078)

github.com/Ceefour18/scRNA_BreastCA
  • Docker-containerised Seurat v5 pipeline (Harmony, scDblFinder, SingleR, sccomp, pseudobulk DESeq2) across 9 patients and 3 subtypes; fibroblasts elevated in TNBC (FDR 0.011); 9 T cell subclusters resolved.

TP53 Cancer Genomics & In Silico Protein Characterisation
